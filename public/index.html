How this project maps to the syllabus

  • Triggers — issuing a book fires trg_before_issue and trg_after_issue; returning a book fires trg_before_return and trg_after_return (auto-calculates a late fine and restocks the copy). All defined in mysql/schema.sql.
  • Views — the Books and Members pages read from available_books_view and member_activity_view.
  • Nested Subqueries & CTEs — see the SQL Reports page: every result there is produced by a MySQL stored procedure containing a real subquery or CTE, called live from the backend API.
